Every feature. One fair subscription.

Monthly and annual plans include every SiteFlow feature. No extra charges to unlock tools. No paid extras.

Planned New Zealand pricing: NZ$12.50 per month or NZ$45 per year, subject to final store price availability. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled. There is no free trial. The store displays your local price and billing period before purchase.

Try an example first

The free demo uses sample photos and text and lets you export an example PDF. Creating and editing your own projects requires a subscription.

Your records remain accessible

After expiry, existing records remain available to view, export and back up. Projects stay on your device; there is no automatic cloud sync. Keep an independent backup. Editing access needs an internet check at least daily. Reports support up to 50 work items, subject to image-size and device limits.
