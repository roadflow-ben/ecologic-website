Privacy policy

Updated 13 November 2026

Who we are

SiteFlow is owned by Eco Logic Ltd, New Zealand. Ben van der Weide is the primary contact. For privacy questions, access or correction requests, email info@ecologic.nz.

Your projects stay on your device

SiteFlow stores project details, customer details you enter, descriptions, branding, photo copies and selections in the app’s storage on your device. It does not upload your projects or photos to Eco Logic Ltd. The app has no account system, advertising, tracking or built-in analytics service.

Photos and permissions

You choose which photos to import. Camera access is used when you choose to take a photo; you can decline access and import existing photos instead. You can change permissions in device settings. Image files may contain embedded information such as capture dates and locations. SiteFlow does not request your device’s location. Backups include the image files stored by the app and may preserve their embedded information.

Sharing and device backups

PDF reports include selected photos in visible placeholders, plus the report text and branding. Portable backups include the whole project: all photo candidates, hidden placeholders and removed work items/photos. Sharing sends a file only to the app, person or storage destination you choose. Those recipients and services handle it under their own policies. Your operating system may also back up app data according to your Apple or Google account settings. Eco Logic Ltd does not control those backups.

Retention and removal

Removing a photo, work item or project hides it but keeps it recoverable on this device. There is no automatic expiry of removed content. New internal backup exports retain two verified copies per project; older legacy backups and databases preserved during recovery may also remain. To erase all local SiteFlow content, use your operating system’s Delete App or clear-app-data option, rather than Offload App. Separately delete exported reports, portable backups and device/cloud backups wherever you saved them. Eco Logic Ltd cannot remotely erase copies held on your device or by recipients.

Keeping your records safe

Use a device passcode and keep an independent backup. SiteFlow relies on the operating system’s storage protections and does not add password encryption to portable backup files or PDFs. Store and share them only where appropriate. “Saved on this device” is not confirmation of an external backup.

When you contact us

If you email support, Eco Logic Ltd receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate the issue and maintain necessary support records, using our email service providers. Supplying this information is voluntary; without enough detail we may not be able to resolve the issue. Please avoid sending customer photos or full backups unless needed and agreed. We retain support information only while needed for support or applicable record-keeping obligations. You may request access, correction or deletion; we will explain any information we need to retain.

Website and platform services

Opening the Eco Logic website or your email app leaves SiteFlow. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process standard connection information such as your IP address. Apple, Google, your email provider and any storage provider you choose operate their own services under their own policies. SiteFlow itself does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Updates and questions

We will update this notice if SiteFlow’s data handling changes. The date above identifies this version. Contact info@ecologic.nz for questions or concerns about Eco Logic Ltd’s handling of your personal information.
